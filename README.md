## Hi, I'm ForgeLab AI 👋

I build **AI automation and AI video pipelines** for small businesses and creators: tools that make, edit and publish content with as little manual work as possible.

I hold a master's degree in software engineering and run a laptop shop, so I work with real hardware every day and build software to save real hours.

### What I build

- **Publishing automation**: one app that uploads and schedules videos to YouTube, Instagram, Facebook and TikTok through their official APIs.
- **AI video generation**: local text-to-video and image-to-video pipelines (ComfyUI, LTX-2) that turn a script into finished, voiced short episodes, running on a 6 GB laptop GPU.
- **Automatic video editing**: scripts that turn raw screen recordings into tutorials with step titles, zooms, speed ramps and loudness-normalized audio (ffmpeg + Python).
- **Workflow automation**: connecting the tools a business already uses so routine work runs by itself.
